# MINT privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

MINT (Make It Nice to Read) is a reader-view extension for Chrome. **It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.**

## What MINT does not do

- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- It makes no network requests. No data leaves your browser.
- It doesn't sync anything to the cloud.
- It doesn't read your browsing history, and it doesn't run in the background on the pages you visit.

## What MINT does

When you click the MINT icon or press its keyboard shortcut, MINT reads the page in the current tab, on your device, to build a clean reader view of the article. That happens only in your browser, and only for the tab you chose. Nothing from the page is saved or sent anywhere. Closing reader view discards it.

MINT saves your display preferences (text size, theme, font, column width and line spacing) with Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They stay on your device and are never synced or transmitted. Uninstalling MINT deletes them.

Images in an article load from the website that published them, exactly as they would on the original page. MINT doesn't contact any server of its own.

## Permissions

- **activeTab**: lets MINT access the current tab only after you click the icon or press the shortcut.
- **scripting**: lets MINT add the reader view to that tab.
- **storage**: saves your display preferences on your device.

MINT asks for no access to websites in general.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. MINT will never start collecting data without a clear notice first.
